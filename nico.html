Project NiCo

In the quest for proving the objective superiority of anarchism in every aspect of conviviality, an example shall be established for people to have the evidence they need to know that with full certainty. As such, a location had to be found that can be self-sufficient, would be willing to adopt anarchy and would not conflict with international affairs significantly.

As of current research, the places that come into question are the sovereign states of Niue and Cook Islands; diplomatically, such an undertaking would be unproblematic due to the way they are bond (or rather not bond) by New Zealand. Put succinctly, they stand in free association with New Zealand but can refrain from it at any time. It is also legally possible to remove money from those islands alongside fully established and guaranteed self-sufficiency altogether, and that would get those islands a lot close to anarchism already.

Niue

This single island is one of two states currently in free association with New Zealand. The place is currently inhabited by a bit under 2000 people and mostly by people speaking Niuean, English and both. Due to the small population and the relative geographic isolation of this nation, establishing a fully functioning anarchy in the near future is not unthinkable.

It is inhabited by around 15000 people, with a vast majority of people speaking the local Maori, English or both.
