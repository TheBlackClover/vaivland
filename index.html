Vaivland

You feel lost in life, stuck in time, forced to work?

Then look no further than Vaivland! We are an anarchist community in [...]. Everyone's welcome to join!

We

  • are anticapitalist,
  • are not ageist,
  • despise all acts of aggression,
  • go against the total and alternativeless dependency on anything or anyone,
  • want everyone to both make experiences and make decisions,
  • go against herd mentality to seek individuality,
  • go against other strict social structures,
  • appreciate creativity,
  • are not perfectionist,
  • support people of all kinds,
  • offer help for all people in dire need
  • and are (f)lawless.

And we can make the "f" happen together!

We don't believe anything because that's what people do in church... We do think though and therefore we are.

In all seriousness, we think that everyone should be unconditionally welcomed, provided with what they need to survive and live a good life and cared about by others. Nobody should be left alone except they wish so for a while. Nobody should be left out! Because a world where everybody can thrive is a world that flourishes the most!

Think of all the great people who suffer from the chronic stress of everyday life and the mundaneness of life under social structures... Think of the people who are pushed to the edge of existence and thus fail to see a reason to keep living... Think of the losses that could've been avoided if we had stayed peaceful, welcoming and non-judgemental all along!
